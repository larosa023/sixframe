How to play

Name the movie, TV show and video game from up to 6 real frames. Each category gets a new puzzle every day at midnight Eastern.

  • Start typing and pick a title from the list.
  • A wrong guess or Skip unlocks the next frame and a new hint.
  • Frame 1 is the hardest shot. Each one after it is easier.
  • You get 6 tries. Tap a frame to see it full screen.
