How to play

Name the movie, TV show or video game from up to 6 frames. There's a new puzzle in each category every day at midnight Eastern.

  • Type a title and pick it from the list.
  • A wrong guess or Skip reveals the next frame and unlocks a hint.
  • Frame 1 is the hardest shot. Each frame after it is easier, and frame 6 is the most iconic.
  • You get 6 guesses in total.

🟩 Correct   🟨 Right franchise   🟥 Wrong   ⬛ Skipped

Example: guessing Toy Story 2 when the answer is Toy Story scores 🟨. It still costs a guess.

Missed a day? Every puzzle from the last 101 days is in the Archive.
